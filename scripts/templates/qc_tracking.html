How to read this

  • Likelihood values are not probabilities. DeepLabCut 3 (PyTorch) likelihoods typically sit around 0.1–0.5 for a correct detection, so a fixed 0.9 threshold (used on the older Streamlit tracking page) rejects most good frames. Compare keypoints and sessions with each other instead, and use the jump and anatomy checks as the evidence of errors.
  • Jumps: frame-to-frame displacement faster than 150 cm/s (converted to pixels with the session's video scale). Warning above 0.5 % of frames, problem above 2 % (any keypoint).
  • Ear swaps: frames where the left ear is on the opposite side of the nose-to-neck axis from where it is in most of the session. Warning above 1 %, problem above 5 %. Swaps flip the ear-based head direction by up to 180°. Because this is relative to the session's own majority, a session labelled left/right the wrong way throughout shows 0 % here; the left ear side column catches that case (fraction of frames with the left ear on the positive side of the axis; flagged when a session falls on the other side of 50 % from most sessions).
  • Ear distance and body length outliers: more than 3 robust SD (3 × 1.4826 median absolute deviations) from the session median; NaN frames count as outliers. About 0.3 % of frames exceed this by chance. Warning above 2 %, problem above 10 %.
  • Nose-to-tail order: projected onto the nose→tail-base axis, keypoints should fall in anatomical order. Warning above 5 %, problem above 15 %.
  • Head keypoints low: frames with at least two of nose, ears and head-midpoint below their 25th-percentile cut-off; those frames carry the least head-direction information.
  • Dark − light: median over keypoints of (median likelihood in dark − in light). Warning below −0.03, problem below −0.08.
  • Brightness step: median, over light switches, of the change in mean frame brightness between the 20 s after and the 20 s before the switch, signed so that positive = brighter with the lights on, relative to the session's median brightness. Comparing just either side of each switch removes the slow decline over the session. Warning above 2 %, problem above 5 % in either direction.
  • Frame viewer: the question for each frame is whether the filled points sit on the right body parts. Open points are expected where a part is hidden (for example under the headstage cable); a filled point off the mouse is a confident error, which the likelihood cut-off does not remove.

Methods

Code and references

Per-session summaries: hm2p.qc.tracking.summarise_tracking, using the checks in hm2p.pose.quality (jumps, ear distance and swaps, body length, anterior-posterior order). Pose files are loaded with the pipeline loader hm2p.kinematics.compute.load_pose_dataset (movement); the champion file is chosen with hm2p.pose.select.select_champion_h5. Light epochs come from the session's kinematics.h5. Data collection: scripts/make_qc_reports.py; page: scripts/build_qc_reports_html.py.
