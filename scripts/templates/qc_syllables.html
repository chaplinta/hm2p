Do syllables differ from each other?

Kinematic signature per syllable

Median body speed and median |angular head velocity| of the frames in each syllable (syllables with ≥ 50 good frames), marker area ∝ usage. Interpretable syllables should spread out (still, turning, running); a cloud collapsed in one corner means the syllables do not separate locomotion.

Usage with lights on vs off

Each dot is a syllable (log axes). Points off the diagonal are used more in one condition. This is behaviour, not a quality failure, but a syllable that exists only in one condition can also reflect tracking differences.

Bout-to-bout transitions (20 most-used syllables)

P(next syllable | current syllable), counted between consecutive bouts, so the diagonal is empty by construction (a frame-level matrix would be dominated by self-transitions). Rows with a few strong entries indicate structured sequences; uniform rows indicate none.

How to read this

  • The first fit (2026-06-01, κ = 10⁶, 10 PCs, no AR-only stage) was judged not usable: two syllables covered about 70 % of frames, 15 % of bouts were one frame long, and the median bout was about 200 ms (docs/kpms-quality-report.md). The fitting script was rewritten on 2026-06-02 (4 PCs, AR-only stage, noise calibration). The provenance panel above shows which fit these files come from.
  • The four criteria are not independent: for usage distributions that fall off as a power law, 20–40 syllables covering 80 % and an entropy ratio of 0.6–0.8 are only met together in a narrow range, so failing one of the two by a small margin is not by itself a reason to reject a fit.
  • Short bouts of a single frame (33 ms at 30 fps) are shorter than any real movement and mostly reflect state flickering at boundaries.
