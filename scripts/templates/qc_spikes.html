How to read this

  • Noise SD: robust dF/F noise from the median absolute deviation of frame-to-frame differences (insensitive to slow drift and sparse transients). SNR: 99th percentile of dF/F divided by the noise SD. Session-median SNR below 5 is a warning, below 3 a problem.
  • V&H vs SD Jaccard: overlap of the two event masks (frames in both / frames in either). Values near 1 mean the detectors agree; below 0.3 is a warning, below 0.15 a problem.
  • V&H events with spikes: fraction of V&H events with at least 0.5 expected CASCADE spikes (CASCADE output summed over the event frames). Below 70 % is a warning, below 50 % a problem: events that CASCADE ignores are either missed spikes or false events.
  • Spike–dF/F ρ: Spearman correlation of the CASCADE rate with dF/F at zero lag. Low values are expected for very sparse cells; a negative value indicates a sign or alignment error.
  • F0 end / start: median of the last 5 % of the baseline over the first 5 %. Below 0.6 (more than 40 % loss) is a problem; it matches the bleaching threshold in hm2p.calcium.qc.
  • CASCADE rate is shown in Hz: CASCADE outputs the expected number of spikes in each frame, which is multiplied by the frame rate here (Rupprecht et al. 2021; CASCADE README). The spikes_units attribute written to ca.h5 by scripts/run_cascade.py says "spikes/s", which does not match the CASCADE output. Inference was run with padding=0, so trace edges are 0 rather than NaN.
