## Tangentially relevant / methods papers

### 1. SLIM-2PF: a miniature two-photon fiberscope for multi-depth neuroimaging in freely moving mice

Authors not fully specified in search results. 2026.
"SLIM-2PF: a miniature two-photon fiberscope for multi-depth neuroimaging in freely
moving mice." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.12.766309v1

**Findings:** Describes a 1.6 g miniature two-photon fiberscope using an electrowetting
lens with high-NA distal optics. The focus is controlled off-head through a thin copper-wire
bundle, minimising probe weight. Lateral and axial resolutions of 0.76–0.81 μm and
6.4–6.8 μm across an 84 μm depth range; field of view 168 μm diameter. Demonstrated
dual-plane simultaneous recording of dendritic and somatic activity at 3 Hz per plane in
mice expressing GCaMP6m in somatosensory cortex pyramidal neurons. First demonstration
of cross-sectional cylindrical-surface imaging in freely moving animals.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Methods context for our head-mounted 2P system. The dual-plane
somatic/dendritic mode is directly relevant — our RSP imaging plane contains both soma
and dendrite ROIs, and this paper provides a proof-of-concept for spatially separating
them optically. The 1.6 g weight is lighter than current generation systems; relevant
context for the methods discussion. No RSP or HD cell content.

### 2. A protocol for cortical and deep-brain calcium imaging in freely moving mice with the Mini2P system

Tello Garcia IA, Alhassen S, Portera-Cailliau C, Zeiger W. 2026.
"A protocol for cortical and deep-brain calcium imaging in freely moving mice with the
Mini2P system." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.09.764980v1

**Findings:** Step-by-step protocol for Mini2P calcium imaging in freely moving mice,
including decision points, validation criteria, and troubleshooting. Covers both cortical
surface and deep-brain (cannula-guided) preparations. Aims to improve reproducibility
across labs adopting the Mini2P platform.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Direct methodological overlap — we use a head-mounted 2P system
(earlier generation than Mini2P but same conceptual platform) in freely moving mice. The
protocol paper provides a useful procedural reference, particularly for handling and motion
correction in freely moving recordings. No RSP or HD cell content. Posted Nov 9, one day
outside the nominal 7-day window, but included given direct relevance.

### 3. Amyloidosis and tauopathy progressively disrupt near-critical dynamics in selectively vulnerable circuits

Authors not fully specified in search results. 2026.
"Amyloidosis and tauopathy progressively disrupt near-critical dynamics in selectively
vulnerable circuits." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.11.766020v1

**Findings:** Simultaneous single-unit recordings in retrosplenial cortex (RSC), anterior
cingulate cortex (ACC), hippocampal CA1, and primary visual cortex in freely behaving
young and old APP/PS1 and wild-type mice. Three criticality-theory measures revealed
deficits that emerged only with age and only in the selectively vulnerable regions (RSC,
ACC, CA1), not in V1. Results replicated in a P301S/E4 tauopathy model.

**Relevance to hm2p:** RSC is directly implicated as one of the selectively vulnerable
regions. The finding establishes a baseline for freely behaving RSC electrophysiology in
normal mice against which pathological changes are measured. The simultaneous RSC + CA1
recording design is relevant background for our decoding analyses (RSC-hippocampal
functional coupling). Not about HD cells, Penk+ neurons, or spatial navigation, so the
connection is indirect. Useful for the discussion of RSC as a circuit hub that integrates
multi-region activity.
