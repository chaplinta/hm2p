## Tangentially relevant / methods papers

### 1. Global structure of relational knowledge networks represented in retrosplenial complex

Feld GB, Fernandez Velasco P, Gahnstrom CJ, Bernard M, Mian Q, Garvert MM, Dick F,
Gerchen MF, Spiers HJ. 2026.
"Global structure of new relational knowledge networks is represented in retrosplenial
complex, and node-distance in hippocampus." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.24.740522v1

**Findings:** Human fMRI study. RSC (retrosplenial complex) encoded the global topological
structure of newly learned abstract relational knowledge graphs, while hippocampal
representations scaled with node-distance within the graph. The double dissociation
suggests RSC holds a summary-level map of relational structure while hippocampus
encodes metric within-graph distances.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Indirect. Spiers lab result confirming RSC as a global-structure
encoder across domains (spatial and non-spatial). For our project, the interest is
whether this graph-like global representation in RSC is substrate-specific (Penk+ vs
non-Penk cells). The finding is consistent with RSC providing a high-level reference
frame — the same circuit role proposed for HD and positional coding in our maze. Human
study, no cellular resolution; cannot directly test cell-type specificity. Relevant
for Discussion framing only.
