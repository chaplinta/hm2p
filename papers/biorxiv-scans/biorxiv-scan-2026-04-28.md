window. Searches continued to return the same set of papers as previous scan cycles. The
most recent RSC/RSP preprints identified across all searches remain the "Active pursuit

processing chains span cytoarchitectures to organize association cortex"
(10.64898/2026.04.21.717753, Gordon et al.) — but it uses human fMRI and concerns
prefrontal association cortex patch organisation unrelated to RSP HD cell types.

**Penk/enkephalin + cortex:** No new preprints. This is the tenth consecutive scan with
no Penk+ cortical navigation paper. The persistent absence is scientifically meaningful:
Penk+ RSP neurons have not been functionally characterised in any published or preprint
study. The most recent Penk preprints remain confined to subcortical circuits: D2-MSN
disinhibition in ventral pallidum (March 11, cocaine abstinence;

3; 10.64898/2026.04.03.716295), and DRN enkephalin neurons in pain/reward modulation
(October 2025).

## Summary of implications for hm2p

**Another quiet window.** No new preprints relevant to the hm2p project were identified
in the April 25–28 period.

**Cumulative picture after ten consecutive scans (April 2–28, 26 days):**

- No study across any scan window has characterised HD tuning properties of
  genetically-defined RSP neuron populations. The Penk+ RSP HD cell literature gap is
  stable and confirmed across ten scan cycles.
- No new paper on RSP HD cells combined with visual landmark removal or light deprivation
  has appeared since the Tian et al. MEC miniature 2P paper (April 2 scan).
- The three most actionable new papers from this scan cycle remain: (1) Laurent/Jacob
  multidirectional cells in RSC (April 2); (2) active locomotion predictively rescues HD
  attractor dynamics (April 6, 10.64898/2026.01.12.698940); (3) causal single-cell RSC
  connectivity during decision-making (April 15).
- The April 21 paper on parallel processing chains in association cortex is the only new
  preprint confirmed to be from this scan's date window; it is not relevant to hm2p.

**No new changes to analysis strategy.** Action items from previous scans still stand:

1. Separate moving vs stationary epochs in all light/dark HD tuning comparisons (motivated
   by the January 2026 active locomotion paper: HD attractor is state-dependent).
2. Document imaging position along the RSC anterior-posterior axis (Wei et al. 2025
   gradient).
3. Consider CEBRA orthogonal subspace test: does light/dark context separate from HD
   coding subspace in population activity?
4. Cite the updated Suite2p preprint in Stage 1 pipeline methods.

**Total new papers this scan: 0.** Cumulative across all scans (April 2–28): approximately
21 papers of varying relevance. The field appears to be between preprint bursts on RSP
spatial coding; no major RSP HD paper has posted to bioRxiv in at least 10 weeks.
