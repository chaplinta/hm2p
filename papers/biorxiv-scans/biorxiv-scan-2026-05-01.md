## Searches with no new relevant results

**Retrosplenial cortex (RSP/RSC):** No new preprints. All search hits returned papers
already captured in prior scans or pre-April-24 submissions. The most recent RSC
preprint from the window was the April 26 paper "Temporal Dynamics of Cortical State
Plasticity Following Adult Vision Loss" (biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.21.719808v1),
which used widefield mesoscopic calcium imaging in adult mice and found that RSC
locomotion-gating is transiently reversed after bilateral enucleation. This is tangentially
relevant (RSC integrates visual and locomotor signals), but the plasticity timescale
(weeks post-enucleation) is not comparable to our 1-minute light-off epochs. No
action items for hm2p.

**Penk/enkephalin + cortex:** No new preprints. Nine consecutive scan cycles with no
Penk+ cortical spatial paper. Results returned only subcortical Penk papers: MPOA-Penk
mating behaviour (April 3); striatal D2-MSN enkephalin + cocaine abstinence (March 11);
DRN-Penk reward modulation (October 2025); Barrington's nucleus Penk + micturition. None
involve cortical Penk neurons in a navigation or spatial context. The genetically defined
Penk+ cortical HD cell literature gap remains open.

**No new relevant preprints found.** The April 24–May 1 window was silent for all

   epochs in all light/dark HD comparisons.

   biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.07.716761v1) — circuit context for Penk+ vs.
   non-Penk RSP roles.
