## Tangentially relevant / methods papers

### 1. A canonical input to the head direction compass carries predominantly eye movement signals

Stott JJ, Chang TY, Bao J, Zhang K, Cullen KE, van der Meer MA. 2026.
"A canonical input to the head direction compass carries predominantly eye movement
signals." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.26.761874v1

Posted: 27 October 2026.

**Findings:** Recordings from the nucleus prepositus hypoglossi (NPH), a brainstem
nucleus classically assumed to relay angular head velocity (AHV) to the head direction
attractor network via the anterodorsal thalamus. The authors report that NPH neurons
predominantly encode eye movements rather than head rotation. Empirically constrained
population models reconstructed eye position and gaze direction; head-direction readouts
failed when eye and head motion were dissociated. This contradicts the standard view
of NPH as a dedicated AHV relay.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Indirect but non-trivial. If NPH is primarily an eye-movement
structure, the idiothetic (path integration) drive to the RSP HD circuit in darkness
must arrive through other channels — potentially the dorsal tegmental nucleus or
lateral mammillary nucleus. For our light-off analysis, this is relevant background:
when visual cues are removed, HD maintenance must rely on AHV integration from
brainstem sources that remain poorly characterised. If confirmed, this result shifts
the circuit picture used when interpreting RSP HD drift in darkness. Not about RSP
directly, not about calcium imaging.

### 2. Manifolds for the neural representation of 3D spatial experience

Doudlah RT, Xiao M, Postlewaite SB, Kim B, Cowan NJ, Rosenberg A. 2026.
"Manifolds for the neural representation of 3D spatial experience." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.22.760836v1

Posted: ~22–23 October 2026.

**Findings:** Proposes a general geometric framework in which neural representations
of pose (position + orientation) lie on manifolds derived from the Special Euclidean
Group SE(3). Applied the framework to head direction limbic systems in fruit bats,
mice, and monkeys, and to 3D object orientation tuning in macaque posterior parietal
cortex. The authors derive a taxonomy of possible population geometry shapes and
provide tools for identifying which manifold type a given population occupies.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Methodologically relevant for population-level analyses in
Stage 6. Our CEBRA embeddings of RSP HD cell populations will produce a latent
geometry; this framework provides theoretical grounding for interpreting that geometry
(what ring or torus topology is expected, how to quantify the match). The mouse HD
data included could serve as a comparison benchmark. Not specific to RSP, not
about calcium imaging, and the project operates in 2D (mice do not navigate
volumetrically), so the 3D aspects are largely irrelevant; the 2D azimuth (HD) case
is the SE(2) limit of their framework, which is well-established. Use as a citation
if we characterise CEBRA embedding topology.
