## Moderately relevant papers

### 1. Vision shapes hippocampal spatial maps through an ancient midbrain pathway

Brenner JM, Ruediger S, Wilhite C, Regalado JM, Senzai Y, Voskobiynyk Y, Paz JT,
Scanziani M, Beltramo R. 2026. "Vision shapes neural maps of space through an ancient
midbrain pathway." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.16.725555v1
(Posted May 16–19, 2026; not indexed in search results until this scan.)

**Findings:** Hippocampal CA1 activity in mice traversing a linear track in alternating
light and dark epochs produced two distinct spatial maps — one active in light, one in
dark. These distinct maps persisted after bilateral ablation of primary visual cortex
(V1), showing that V1 is not required. Blocking the pathway from superior colliculus
(SC) to lateral visual cortex (LV) — an ancient, evolutionarily conserved visual relay
not previously associated with spatial navigation — markedly reduced the difference
between light and dark maps. The SC→LV pathway conveys visual information via widefield
units sensitive to moving stimuli and visual flow.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Directly addresses the circuit basis of visually modulated spatial
representations in the alternating light/dark paradigm — the same light-off/on design
we use in the rose maze. Two implications for our project: (1) HD tuning changes
observed in RSP during lights-off may not depend on V1 inputs but could instead be
driven by SC→LV→RSP projections; this is relevant for the Discussion when
characterising which visual pathway gates RSP HD anchoring. (2) The finding that
hippocampal light vs dark maps are dissociable is consistent with our expectation that
RSP HD tuning will also dissociate — but this paper shows the effect is subcortically
mediated, which makes it less straightforward to attribute RSP light/dark differences to
simple V1 input loss. If similar non-V1 visual pathways gate RSP HD anchoring in Penk+
cells, a future experiment with SC or LV lesions would be informative. Not in RSP;
comparison point only.

## Summary

**Total new relevant preprints this scan:** 1 (Brenner et al. 2026, moderately relevant).

Today's scan surfaced one paper missed in prior scans due to search indexing lag: Brenner
et al. 2026, which uses the same light/dark alternation paradigm we use and identifies
an SC→lateral visual cortex pathway as the key circuit driving visual modulation of
hippocampal spatial maps. This constrains how we interpret RSP HD tuning changes during
lights-off: V1 suppression alone is not the likely mechanism; SC-mediated visual pathways
should be considered.

The volumetric two-photon paper from May 28 remains unconfirmed pending manual retrieval.

**Cumulative picture (April 2–June 3, 63 days, 34 scans):**

No preprint has characterised HD tuning of genetically-defined RSP excitatory neuron
populations (Penk+ or Penk⁻CamKII+) across any scan window. The four most recent
substantive preprints within the active monitoring window:

- **Oh et al. 2026** (May 10): RSC PV/SST interneurons and egocentric spatial coding
  precision/stability. PV cells govern egocentric coding precision via motion-linked
  bearing-aligned synchrony; SST cells govern long-term global stability via
  boundary-anchored activity. Optogenetic silencing confirms independent roles. Informs
  Discussion framing of Penk+ vs Penk⁻CamKII+ HD differences: excitatory cell-type
  differences are likely shaped by differential PV/SST gate state.
  https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.10.724096v1

- **Coordinated representational drift across mouse cortex** (May 5): RSC confirmed as
  highest-density spatially tuned region in dorsal cortex alongside V1; population
  manifold geometry mirrors maze structure. Coordinated multi-region drift across a
  47-day longitudinal imaging dataset; within-session stability substantially higher
  than cross-session. Relevant for reporting HD tuning stability estimates.
  https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.05.723038v1

- **Prankerd et al. 2026** (May 15): coppaFISH 3D volumetric in situ sequencing +
  CASTalign computational pipeline for post-hoc transcriptomic cell-type assignment
  to in vivo 2P-imaged neurons. Relevant as a future validation or follow-up tool for
  confirming viral cell-type labels in our dataset.
  https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.15.725413v1

- **Brenner et al. 2026** (May 16–19): SC→lateral visual cortex pathway drives
  hippocampal light/dark spatial map dissociation; V1 ablation does not abolish the
  effect. Constrains interpretation of RSP HD tuning changes in darkness — V1 loss of
  input is not the primary mechanism; subcortical SC relay should be considered.
  https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.16.725555v1
