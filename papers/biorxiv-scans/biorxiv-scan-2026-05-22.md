## Summary

**Total new relevant preprints this scan:** 0.

May 22 (Friday) shows no new bioRxiv postings in any monitored topic area relevant to

**Cumulative picture (April 2–May 22, 51 days, 29 scans):**

The literature gap on genetically-defined RSP excitatory neuron populations persists.
No preprint has characterised HD tuning of Penk+ or Penk⁻CamKII+ RSP neurons. The
three most substantive recent preprints in the active monitoring window remain:

- **Oh et al. 2026** (May 10): RSC PV/SST interneuron dissociation. PV cells govern
  egocentric coding precision via motion-linked bearing-aligned synchrony; SST cells
  govern long-term global stability via boundary-anchored activity. Optogenetic
  silencing confirms independent roles. Provides local circuit context: excitatory
  cell-type tuning differences (Penk+ vs Penk⁻CamKII+) are likely shaped by
  differential PV/SST inhibitory gating.

- **Coordinated representational drift across mouse cortex** (May 5): RSC confirmed
  as highest-density spatially tuned region in dorsal cortex. Population manifold
  geometry mirrors maze structure. Coordinated multi-region drift across 47-day
  longitudinal imaging; within-session stability substantially higher than cross-session.

- **Prankerd et al. 2026** (May 15): coppaFISH 3D volumetric in situ sequencing +
  CASTalign for post-hoc transcriptomic cell-type assignment to in vivo 2P-imaged
  neurons. Potential validation or follow-up tool for confirming viral cell-type labels
  in our dataset.
