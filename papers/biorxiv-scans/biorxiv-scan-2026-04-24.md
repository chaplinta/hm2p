## Note on April 17–22 coverage

During today's broader search sweep (covering April 17–24 to cross-check prior scans),
one paper posted April 17, 2026 was identified that falls in the April 19 scan window
and should have been captured there:

**Damphousse CC, Calvin OL, Redish AD. 2026.
"Hippocampal representations differentiate reactive and anticipatory responses during
foraging under threat." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.17.719234v1**

This paper records CA1 place cells in rats performing a predator-based foraging task and
shows that hippocampal representations during reactive retreats remain anchored to the
attack location, while anticipatory pause states show distinct population dynamics. Not
directly relevant to RSP or HD tuning — included here only for completeness. If anything,
it is tangentially relevant as an example of spatial coding in freely moving animals
under dynamic threat conditions. No action items for hm2p.
