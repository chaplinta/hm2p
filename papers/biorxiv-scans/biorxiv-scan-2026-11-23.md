**Active action items (from prior scans, not yet superseded):**
- Zhang et al. (Cell Reports, 2026-11-11, covered 2026-11-12.md): Check whether the
  two RSC directional code types (global azimuthal HD vs. geometry-referenced direction
  specific to the curved-surface geometry) are differentially expressed across Penk+ and
  Penk⁻CamKII+ populations in the rose-maze data.
- Duszkiewicz et al. (2026-11-06, covered 2026-11-13.md): Analyse AHV tuning strength
  separately for Penk+ and Penk⁻CamKII+ cells. Weaker AHV tuning in Penk+ would, under
  the visual-capture framework, predict stronger visual anchoring in light and larger
  preferred-direction drift in dark epochs.

**Persistent gap:** No group has characterised genetically defined RSP subpopulations
(Penk+, CamKII intersectional, or any other molecular identity) in an HD or spatial
navigation context. This absence is unchanged across all 2026 scans.
