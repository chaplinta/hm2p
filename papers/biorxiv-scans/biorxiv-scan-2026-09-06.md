## Tangentially relevant / methods papers

### 1. Brainwide representation of navigation

Authors not confirmed from search results. 2026.
"Brainwide representation of navigation." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.29.747979v1
Submitted: 2026-08-29. Newly indexed: 2026-09-06.

**Findings:** Recorded from over 20,000 neurons in mice navigating a virtual corridor that
decoupled spatial position from correlated signals (running speed, visual flow, reward). Spatial
position was encoded in every sampled brain region, not only in the hippocampal formation. Neurons
tuned to visual landmarks showed more position coding than non-landmark-tuned neurons across all
regions. The hippocampal formation represented spatial position slightly more uniformly than other
regions but less precisely than visual cortex. Running speed and reward modulation were widespread
brainwide.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Tangential. The result that visual landmark tuning is a general predictor
of position coding, rather than a hippocampal-exclusive property, is consistent with RSP landmark
coding. It does not address HD tuning, cell-type identity, or light/dark manipulation. The virtual
corridor design limits comparability to our freely-moving rose maze paradigm. Useful background
for framing the population-level brainwide context (RSP is one node in a distributed spatial
coding network), but does not directly constrain interpretation of Penk+ vs. non-Penk HD
differences. Note that no RSP-specific analysis or cell-type breakdown is reported in the abstract.

   **Priority: medium.**

   subspace? Does the RSP population manifold mirror the rose maze graph topology?
   Cite Feld/Spiers et al. 2026 and Peters et al. 2026.
   **Priority: medium.**

5. Frame excitatory cell-type HD differences in the Discussion using the PV-precision /
   SST-stability inhibitory dissociation (Oh et al. 2026): differences in Penk+ vs.
   non-Penk HD tuning downstream of shared PV sharpening imply excitatory input differences
   or intrinsic biophysical properties, not differential inhibition.
   **Priority: medium.**

   imperfect, low-amplitude dF/F events in apparent soma ROIs may reflect subthreshold

   for low-firing non-Penk cells where HD tuning is weaker.
   **Priority: medium.**

7. Note coordinated representational drift result (Peters et al. 2026) when reporting
   session-wise HD tuning stability — within-session estimates are more reliable than
   cross-session comparisons.
   **Priority: low.**

   **Priority: watch.**
