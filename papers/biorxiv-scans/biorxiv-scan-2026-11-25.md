## Tangentially relevant / methods papers

### 1. SLIM-2PF: a miniature two-photon fiberscope for multi-depth neuroimaging in freely moving mice

Authors not specified in search results. 2026.
"SLIM-2PF: a miniature two-photon fiberscope for multi-depth neuroimaging in freely
moving mice." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.12.766309v1

**Note:** Posted 2026-11-16, which falls just outside the strict seven-day window and
within the previous scan's window (2026-11-07 to 2026-11-24). It was not retrieved by
the 2026-11-24 scan, so it is included here as a catch-up.

**Findings:** Describes a 1.6 g two-photon fiberscope in which the focus is controlled
off-head via a copper-wire bundle, minimising the mass attached to the animal. The system
supports dual-plane recording across a 60 µm depth span at up to 4 Hz per plane. Tested
in freely moving mice expressing GCaMP6m.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Tangential — our existing head-mounted 2P system is already
deployed. SLIM-2PF is in a similar technical category and extends the class of
hardware available for head-direction and maze navigation studies in freely moving mice.
The off-head focus control approach is distinct from MINI2P-II (mass reduction by
footprint compression) and may offer a path to lighter head stages in future
experiments. No relevance to our current analysis but useful context for the methods
discussion.
