## Tangentially relevant / methods papers

### 1. Projection-specific filtering of delta-theta input by layer 5 neurons in secondary visual cortex

Takemoto M, Ye Q, Rancz EA. 2026.
"Projection-specific filtering of delta-theta input by layer 5 neurons in mouse secondary
visual cortex." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.06.763938v1

**Findings:** Recorded from layer 5 neurons in mouse medial secondary visual cortex (V2M),
comparing intratelencephalic (IT) and extratelencephalic (ET) projection subtypes. IT cells
act as low-pass filters for subthreshold input in the delta-theta range, while ET cells show
resonance near 3 Hz and 5 Hz. Once firing, the two subtypes show no detectable difference
in spike timing. The result suggests that IT and ET cells differentially gate slow rhythmic
(theta-band) inputs from the cortical hierarchy.

**Relevance to hm2p:** RSP receives substantial visual input from secondary visual areas
(V2/AM/PM). The differential slow-rhythm filtering by V2 IT (corticocortical) vs ET
(corticothalamic/corticospinal) projections could affect what visual information is
relayed to RSP during light-on epochs. This is tangential — the paper does not address
spatial coding, HD, or RSP — but informs the mechanistic understanding of how visual
cortex transmits rhythmically modulated signals to downstream regions. Worth a one-line
citation if we discuss the V2→RSP visual input pathway.
