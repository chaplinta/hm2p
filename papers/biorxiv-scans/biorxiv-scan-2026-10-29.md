## Tangentially relevant / methods papers

### 1. Multi-region two-photon calcium imaging in freely moving animals

Authors not fully specified in search results. 2026.
"Multi-region two-photon calcium imaging in freely moving animals." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.27.760324v1

Posted: 27 October 2026.

**Findings:** Describes MINI2P-II, an implantable periscope lens system enabling
simultaneous two-photon calcium imaging from two spatially separated brain regions in
freely moving mice. Validated by demonstrating inter-hemisphere coordination in
bilateral motor cortex during climbing, and egocentric boundary coding in simultaneous
retrosplenial cortex (RSC) and posterior parietal cortex (PPC) recordings during
open-field navigation.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Two connections. First, the technology is an advance over
earlier-generation head-mounted 2P systems (including the one used for the hm2p
dataset) and is worth noting in the methods/discussion section as future-directions
context. Second, and more directly, the paper reports RSC egocentric boundary coding
in freely moving mice using calcium imaging — the same preparation type as hm2p. Their
RSC data provide a baseline reference for what RSC coding looks like in this
preparation. The egocentric boundary signal in RSC also intersects with HD tuning
(boundary-vector cells have a directional component), so this is relevant background
for interpreting any egocentric coding components we observe in our dataset. Note
that the abstract did not provide author names or full methods — details should be
verified directly on bioRxiv once network access is available.
