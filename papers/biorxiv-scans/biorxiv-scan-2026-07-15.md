## Highly relevant papers

### 1. Diversity and sensorimotor specialization of head direction cells in the mouse thalamus

Hijazi S, Jiang S, Wülfing MS, Quach J, Lachance PA, Hasselmo ME, Viney TJ. 2026.
"Diversity and sensorimotor specialization of head direction cells in the mouse
thalamus." Current Biology.
https://www.cell.com/current-biology/fulltext/S0960-9822(26)00631-7

Published June 10, 2026. bioRxiv preprint (September 8, 2025):
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.09.08.674912v1

Note: the preprint predates the April 2, 2026 start of active daily scanning, and the
journal publication was not surfaced by prior daily scans (all June scans reported zero
new relevant papers). Flagged today on re-search of head-direction + thalamus +
calretinin.

**Findings:** Extracellular recording + juxtacellular labelling + immunohistochemistry
of single HD cells in the anterodorsal nucleus of the thalamus (ADn) in awake mice.
ADn HD cells are not a homogeneous population. A mediolateral gradient of calretinin
(CR)-expressing cells was identified: CR+ cells occupy medial ADn and have narrower
directional tuning widths, lower peak firing rates, and fewer spikes per rebound burst
than CR⁻ cells in lateral ADn. Three projection types were distinguished — Type I (axon
to RSC only), Type II (RSC + dorsomedial striatum collaterals), Type III (tortuosa
morphology, descending axons that avoid the thalamic reticular nucleus). In vivo
responses of individual HD cells are differentially modulated by light pulses, sound, and
movement, confirming sensorimotor specialisation at the thalamic level. Published as the
peer-reviewed version of the "Granularity of thalamic HD cells" preprint.

**Relevance to hm2p:** ADn is the primary thalamic HD source for RSC. This paper
establishes that the HD signal arriving in RSC from ADn is already heterogeneous — CR+
cells deliver sharper, lower-rate HD signals while CR⁻ cells deliver broader, higher-rate
signals. If Penk+ and Penk⁻CamKII+ excitatory populations in RSC receive differential
inputs from these ADn subtypes, that would provide a thalamic-level circuit explanation
for any tuning-width differences we observe between the two populations. Type III cells
(tortuosa, descend without TRN collateral) may represent a distinct modulatory pathway
to RSC that bypasses normal thalamic gating. The sensorimotor specialisation finding
also means that HD cells in ADn respond differently to visual input vs. self-motion — the
proportional shift in light-off conditions in our paradigm may affect which ADn cell type
dominates RSC drive at any given moment. This should be discussed alongside the
Margetts-Smith et al. 2025 result that all RSC pyramidal neurons receive ATN input
(ubiquitous drive does not preclude differential weighting from CR+ vs CR⁻ subtypes).

- **Goodwin, Dombeck et al. 2026** (July 3): ACh release in dRSC coordinates
  representational adaptation during contextual uncertainty. Relevant for interpreting
  tuning dynamics at light-off transitions — predicts a transient tuning-degradation
  peak in the first 30 s of each dark epoch. Captured in the 2026-07-10 scan.
  https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.03.736438

- **Oh et al. 2026** (May 10): RSC PV/SST interneurons and egocentric spatial coding
  precision/stability. PV cells govern egocentric coding precision via motion-linked
  bearing-aligned synchrony; SST cells govern long-term global stability via
  boundary-anchored activity. Informs Discussion framing of Penk+ vs Penk⁻CamKII+ HD
  differences: excitatory cell-type differences are likely shaped by differential
  PV/SST gate state.
  https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.10.724096v1

- **Coordinated representational drift across mouse cortex** (May 5): RSC confirmed as
  highest-density spatially tuned region in dorsal cortex alongside V1; population
  manifold geometry mirrors maze structure. Coordinated multi-region drift across a
  47-day longitudinal imaging dataset; within-session stability substantially higher
  than cross-session. Relevant for reporting HD tuning stability estimates.
  https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.05.723038v1

- **Prankerd et al. 2026** (May 15): coppaFISH 3D volumetric in situ sequencing +
  CASTalign computational pipeline for post-hoc transcriptomic cell-type assignment
  to in vivo 2P-imaged neurons. Relevant as a future validation tool for confirming
  viral cell-type labels in our dataset.
  https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.15.725413v1

- **Hijazi, Viney et al. 2026** (Current Biology, June 10): Diversity and sensorimotor
  specialisation of HD cells in mouse ADn thalamus. CR+/CR⁻ gradient sets distinct
  tuning-width and rate profiles; three projection types to RSC and striatum. Informs
  circuit-level interpretation of RSC excitatory population HD tuning differences.
  https://www.cell.com/current-biology/fulltext/S0960-9822(26)00631-7
