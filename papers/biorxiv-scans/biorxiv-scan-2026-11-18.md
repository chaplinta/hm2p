## Tangentially relevant / methods papers

### 1. SLIM-2PF: a miniature two-photon fiberscope for multi-depth neuroimaging in freely moving mice

Authors not fully specified in search results. 2026.
"SLIM-2PF: a miniature two-photon fiberscope for multi-depth neuroimaging in freely
moving mice." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.12.766309v1

**Findings:** A 1.6 g miniature two-photon fiberscope using an electrowetting lens for
axial focus control off-head, minimising probe weight. Achieves 0.76–0.81 μm lateral
and 6.4–6.8 μm axial resolution over an 84 μm depth range; 168 μm diameter field of
view. Demonstrated dual-plane simultaneous somatic and dendritic recording at 3 Hz per
plane in somatosensory cortex neurons expressing GCaMP6m. First demonstration of
cross-sectional cylindrical-surface imaging in freely moving animals.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Methods context for our head-mounted 2P system. Dual-plane
somatic/dendritic recording is directly pertinent — our RSP imaging plane contains both
soma and dendrite ROIs. SLIM-2PF provides a proof-of-concept for spatially separating
compartments optically, which we currently do post-hoc by ROI classification. No RSP or
HD cell content.

### 2. A protocol for cortical and deep-brain calcium imaging in freely moving mice with the Mini2P system

Tello Garcia IA, Alhassen S, Portera-Cailliau C, Zeiger W. 2026.
"A protocol for cortical and deep-brain calcium imaging in freely moving mice with the
Mini2P system." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.09.764980v1

**Findings:** Step-by-step protocol for Mini2P calcium imaging in freely moving mice,
including decision points, validation criteria, and troubleshooting for both cortical
surface and deep-brain (cannula-guided) preparations. Addresses behavioral habituation,
task integration, acquisition settings, and motion correction. Posted Nov 9 (2 days
outside the nominal 7-day window, included given direct methodological overlap).

**Relevance to hm2p:** We use a head-mounted 2P system built on the same conceptual
platform in freely moving mice. The protocol provides a reference for handling, motion
correction, and QC for freely moving calcium imaging. No RSP or HD cell content.

### 3. Amyloidosis and tauopathy progressively disrupt near-critical dynamics in selectively vulnerable circuits

Authors not fully specified in search results. 2026.
"Amyloidosis and tauopathy progressively disrupt near-critical dynamics in selectively
vulnerable circuits." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.11.766020v1

**Findings:** Simultaneous single-unit recordings in RSC, anterior cingulate cortex
(ACC), hippocampal CA1, and primary visual cortex in freely behaving young and old
APP/PS1 and WT mice. Three criticality-theory measures revealed age-dependent deficits
confined to selectively vulnerable regions (RSC, ACC, CA1); V1 was spared. Results
replicated in a P301S/E4 tauopathy model.

**Relevance to hm2p:** RSC is one of the recorded regions, and this paper establishes a
baseline for freely behaving RSC electrophysiology in normal mice against which
pathological changes are measured. Tangentially relevant as background for the
discussion of RSC's role as a multi-region integration hub. Not about HD tuning,
Penk+ neurons, or spatial navigation.
