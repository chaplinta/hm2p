## Summary of implications for hm2p

**Another quiet day.** No new preprints relevant to the hm2p project were found in
the April 25–26 period.

**Cumulative picture after ten consecutive scans (April 2–26, 24 days):**

- No study across any of the ten scan windows has characterised HD tuning properties of
  genetically-defined RSP neuron populations. The literature gap is stable and confirmed
  across a 24-day continuous monitoring period.
- No paper combining RSP, head direction cells, and light/dark visual cue manipulation
  has appeared in any scan. The experimental niche — cell-type-specific HD tuning under
  controlled visual cue removal, two-photon calcium imaging, freely moving mice — remains
  specific to our study.
- The Penk+ cortex navigation literature continues to be entirely absent. All Penk
  preprints in this period address subcortical circuits (striatum, DRN, MPOA). This
  makes our cell-type identity claim (Penk+ as a distinct RSP HD population) a genuine
  novelty with no direct competition.
- The three most actionable papers identified across this entire scan cycle remain:
  (1) Laurent/Jacob et al. multidirectional cells in RSC (April 2 scan);
  (2) Ioffe et al. all-optical RSC connectivity modulation (April 15 scan);
  (3) Active locomotion HD attractor rescue (April 6 scan).
  No new additions in the past five scans.
