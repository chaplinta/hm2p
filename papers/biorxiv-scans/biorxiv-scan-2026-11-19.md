## Moderately relevant papers

### 1. Amyloidosis and tauopathy progressively disrupt near-critical dynamics in selectively vulnerable circuits

Authors not fully specified in search results. 2026.
"Amyloidosis and tauopathy progressively disrupt near-critical dynamics in selectively
vulnerable circuits." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.11.766020v1

**Findings:** Recorded single units simultaneously from retrosplenial cortex (RSC),
anterior cingulate cortex (ACC), CA1, and primary visual cortex in freely behaving
young and old APP/PS1 (amyloidosis) and P301S/E4 (tauopathy) mice. Both pathologies
caused progressive loss of near-critical dynamics — a statistical signature of network
activity — in the selectively vulnerable circuits (RSC, ACC, CA1). V1 was relatively
spared. The near-criticality loss was age-dependent and converged across two molecularly
unrelated pathologies.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Not directly relevant to our scientific question (Penk+ vs
non-Penk HD tuning in healthy mice). However, the paper provides single-unit data from
freely behaving mice with RSC recordings, which is a comparator for what healthy RSC
dynamics look like. The near-criticality framework could in principle be applied to our
RSC population data as an independent measure of network state. The simultaneous RSC +
CA1 + V1 recording design is methodologically interesting for future multi-region
extensions of hm2p.

## Tangentially relevant / methods papers

### 2. SLIM-2PF: a miniature two-photon fiberscope for multi-depth neuroimaging in freely moving mice

Tian J, Liu Y, Lai C-H, Lu H, Li X. 2026.
"SLIM-2PF: a miniature two-photon fiberscope for multi-depth neuroimaging in freely
moving mice." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.12.766309v1

**Findings:** Presents a lightweight two-photon fiberscope supporting two modes on a
single probe: (1) dual-plane recording of distal apical dendrites and somata at 3 Hz per
plane simultaneously; (2) cross-sectional cylindrical imaging spanning 60 μm depth at up
to 4 Hz. Tested in GCaMP6m-expressing somatosensory cortex pyramidal neurons in freely
moving mice. The cylindrical scanning mode is claimed as the first demonstration in a
freely moving animal. Achieves subcellular resolution.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Our hm2p imaging system is a head-mounted 2P microscope for
freely moving mice. SLIM-2PF's dual-plane mode is relevant to our soma vs dendrite ROI
classification problem — it offers direct simultaneous access to both compartments, which
would eliminate the need for post-hoc morphological classification. The 3 Hz per plane
frame rate is lower than our system (~9.6 Hz single plane). Useful reference for the
discussion section on future directions and technical limitations.

### 3. A protocol for cortical and deep-brain calcium imaging in freely moving mice with the Mini2P system

Tello Garcia IA, Alhassen S, Portera-Cailliau C, Zeiger W. 2026.
"A protocol for cortical and deep-brain calcium imaging in freely moving mice with the
Mini2P system." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.09.764980v1

(Posted 2026-11-11, just at the edge of the 7-day window; included for completeness.)

**Findings:** Step-by-step protocol for implementing the Mini2P system (Zong et al.
2022, Nature Methods) in freely moving mice, covering cortical and deep-brain
applications. Provides decision points, validation criteria, and troubleshooting guidance.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Directly relevant to our imaging pipeline. Mini2P is the same
class of head-mounted two-photon system we use. This protocol paper could inform
troubleshooting and standardisation of our Stage 1 (calcium extraction) procedures. The
deep-brain section is relevant if future experiments target hippocampus or ATN.
