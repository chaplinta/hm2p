## 7-day window caveat

Web search does not reliably index bioRxiv papers within ~3–7 days of posting, and
the current index appears to have a lag of approximately 4–6 weeks for this search
engine. The absence of October 2026 papers does not confirm a quiet period — it
reflects the indexing lag. Manual checking of the bioRxiv neuroscience new-submissions
page (biorxiv.org/collection/neuroscience) for October 10–17 is recommended if
complete coverage is required. The hm2p bioRxiv scan schedule would benefit from
an alternative retrieval method (e.g. the bioRxiv RSS feed, the public API at
api.biorxiv.org, or email alerts) that is not subject to this lag.
