**Cumulative picture (April 2–May 23, 52 days, 29 scans):**

The gap in the literature on genetically-defined RSP excitatory neuron populations
persists. No preprint has characterised HD tuning of Penk+ or Penk⁻CamKII+ RSP
neurons. The three most relevant recent preprints in the active 7-day window remain:

- **Oh et al. 2026** (May 10): RSC PV/SST interneuron dissociation. PV cells govern
  egocentric coding precision via motion-linked bearing-aligned synchrony; SST cells
  govern long-term global stability via boundary-anchored activity. Optogenetic
  silencing confirms independent roles. Provides local circuit context for interpreting
  Penk+ vs Penk⁻CamKII+ HD differences: excitatory cell-type tuning differences are
  likely shaped by differential PV/SST gate state.

- **Coordinated representational drift across mouse cortex** (May 5): RSC confirmed
  as highest-density spatially tuned region in dorsal cortex alongside V1. Population
  manifold geometry mirrors maze structure. Coordinated multi-region drift across
  47-day longitudinal imaging; within-session stability substantially higher than
  cross-session.

- **Prankerd et al. 2026** (May 15): coppaFISH 3D volumetric in situ sequencing +
  CASTalign pipeline for post-hoc transcriptomic cell-type assignment to in vivo
  2P-imaged neurons. Relevant as a validation tool for confirming viral cell-type labels.
