**Cumulative picture (April 2–May 20, 49 days, 27 scans):**

The gap in literature on genetically-defined RSP excitatory neuron populations persists
across all scan windows. No preprint has characterised HD tuning of Penk+ or
Penk⁻CamKII+ RSP neurons. The two most relevant recent preprints remain:

- **Oh et al. 2026** (May 10): RSC PV/SST interneuron dissociation — PV interneurons
  govern egocentric coding precision (motion-linked, bearing-aligned synchrony); SST
  interneurons govern long-term representation stability (boundary-anchored, globally
  coherent dynamics). Optogenetic silencing confirms dissociable roles. Constrains
  interpretation of Penk+ vs Penk⁻CamKII+ HD differences: any cell-type tuning
  difference likely arises at the excitatory level, gated by local PV/SST context.
  https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.10.724096v1

- **Coordinated representational drift / RSC spatial manifold paper** (May 5, first
  captured May 16 scan): RSC confirmed as highest-density spatially tuned region in
  dorsal cortex; population manifold mirrors maze geometry.

- **Prankerd et al. 2026** (May 15): coppaFISH 3D + CASTalign — post-hoc transcriptomic
  cell-type assignment to in vivo two-photon imaged neurons. Relevant as a validation
  tool for our viral cell-type labelling; first captured May 18 scan.
  https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.15.725413v1
