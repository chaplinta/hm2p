## Highly relevant papers

### 1. MINI2P-II: Multi-region two-photon calcium imaging in freely moving animals

Zong W, Gahr M, et al. 2026.
"Multi-region two-photon calcium imaging in freely moving animals." bioRxiv.
https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.27.760324v1

Posted: 27–28 October 2026.

**Findings:** Introduces MINI2P-II, a 1.1 g head-mounted two-photon miniscope
with dual-scope capability for simultaneous recording from two widely separated or
adjacent brain regions. Key demonstrations include: (1) inter-hemisphere coordination
in bilateral motor cortex during a climbing task; (2) simultaneous retrosplenial
cortex (RSC) and posterior parietal cortex (PPC) recordings showing egocentric
boundary coding in freely moving mice. A periscope lens assembly enables deep
(>820 µm) and dual-colour imaging. The system is available for non-commercial
academic use.

**Relevance to hm2p:** Directly relevant on two levels. First, methodological: our
project uses a head-mounted 2P system (earlier generation). MINI2P-II represents
the current state of the art in this technology class and is a direct reference for
positioning our methodology. Its dual-scope capacity is a future direction — we
currently image single-plane RSC, but simultaneous RSC + PPC would be a natural
extension. Second, scientific: the simultaneous RSC + PPC egocentric boundary coding
result demonstrates that RSC spatial cell types (specifically boundary cells) are
accessible with this approach in freely moving mice. Boundary coding coexists with
HD coding in RSC — our rose-maze data should be examined for egocentric boundary
cells in both Penk+ and Penk⁻CamKII+ populations. Cite in the methods section
when discussing 2P miniscope approaches and in the discussion when contextualising
future multi-region work.
