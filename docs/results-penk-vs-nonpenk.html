Penk+ and Penk⁻CamKII+ neurons in the Rosenberg maze

Two-photon GCaMP7f imaging in freely moving mice, 1 min light / 1 min dark, plus ex vivo whole-cell recordings. Analyses run 2026-09-25 to 2026-10-02. Exploratory results, not peer reviewed. Build 2026-10-02 · 891418b.

Penk+ (Cre-ON, 11 animals) Penk⁻CamKII+ (Cre-OFF, 4 animals)

Summary

The two populations differ in how they fire and in what they respond to: Penk+ cells fire long, rare bursts while the mouse runs, and Penk⁻CamKII+ cells follow the room lights more closely.

  • Within Penk+Penk+ calcium events occur during running and turning, follow the run/still state rather than speed, and last through the run (animal-level Wilcoxon p = 0.001–0.004).
  • Between groupsRank composites: running coupling higher in Penk+ (exact permutation p = 0.037, CLES 0.82); light coupling higher in Penk⁻CamKII+ (p = 0.009, CLES 0.86). Defined after the individual measures were seen, so exploratory.
  • Between groupsPenk+ events are longer and rarer (duration 3.3 vs 2.6 s, interval 31 vs 25 s). Isolated small events decay identically, so this is not slower indicator clearance.
  • Ex vivoPenk+ cells have narrower spikes, higher maximal firing and a burst-then-adapt pattern. Cell types come from largely separate mice, so this cannot be separated from animal identity.
  • Bounded nullsHead-direction tuning, population HD decoding, encoding models, syllables, egocentric and navigational coding do not differ. With 11 vs 4 animals, effects below about d = 1.5 would usually be missed.

The two populations are never in the same animal, so inference is at the animal level. Each between-group result is reported as a descriptive cell-level test, an animal-level Mann-Whitney U, an animal-level permutation test, and a leave-one-animal-out (LOAO) direction check. Effect size is the common-language effect size, CLES = P(Penk+ animal > Penk⁻CamKII+ animal); 0.5 means no difference. With 11 vs 4 animals an exact two-sided animal-level test cannot go below p = 0.0015, and 80 % power needs about d = 1.5.

Chart statistics are recomputed from animal medians (two-sided exact Mann-Whitney U). Tables report the programme's own tests, which summarise each animal by its cell mean, so the two can differ.

Not the low-rheobase type. The phenotype moves in the direction of the RSP low-rheobase cells of Brennan et al. The working description is a smaller, narrower-spiking layer 2/3 pyramid that fires a burst and then adapts.

  • Indicator kinetics are equal. Isolated small events decay with the same time constant (median 0.31 vs 0.31 s; brightness-matched 0.34 vs 0.35 s), so longer Penk+ events reflect longer firing episodes.
  • Penk+ cells are about twice as bright at baseline (84 vs 37 raw F), most likely from the different Cre-ON and Cre-OFF constructs.
  • Duration and interval keep their direction on rate-matched and brightness-matched cells (CLES 0.70–0.82) but lose significance.
  • Amplitude and rate are tied to expression. The amplitude difference comes from the four brightest Penk+ animals (median amplitude about 1; the other seven overlap Penk⁻CamKII+), and within Penk+ brighter cells get lower inferred rates (ρ = −0.42). "Penk+ cells fire less" is treated as unconfirmed.

Brightness does not explain the running effect: within Penk+ the running index barely tracks baseline fluorescence (ρ = 0.11), and it holds on brightness-matched cells (p = 0.003). 5. Composite scores

Averaging ranks across measures, both differences reach p < 0.05 at the animal level, with every measure leaning the same way.

Composite rank per animal (higher = more coupled)

For each measure the animal median is oriented so that higher means more coupled, ranked across the 15 animals, and averaged. The two composites are uncorrelated across animals (ρ = −0.08), so they describe separate properties, and both directions hold within each equipment configuration. With a Bonferroni correction for two composites, permutation p is 0.074 (running) and 0.018 (light). The definitions are now fixed for testing in new animals.

One heterogeneity result survives removal of animal means: Penk+ cells are more dispersed in feature space within animals (dispersion ratio 0.80, p = 0.003), the opposite of the expectation that a single transcriptomic type would be the more compact population.

Interpretation

  • Penk+ as a sparse, sustained run-state population. Seconds-long events that coincide with running, follow the run/still state and persist through a run fit cells that report "the animal is running" rather than a continuous variable. A 3 s event is about the length of one corridor run.
  • Penk⁻CamKII+ as more visually coupled. This fits a mixture of excitatory types that includes cells receiving visual or landmark input, as reported for dysgranular RSP (Jacob et al. 2017). Because the composites were defined post hoc and the groups were labelled with different constructs, this is a lead to confirm.
  • Burst firing and enkephalin (speculative). Burst-then-adapt firing produces clustered spikes at the start of a drive, the mode generally needed for neuropeptide release, so Penk+ cells could release enkephalin during runs. Nothing here measures peptide release.

Limitations

  • 11 vs 4 animals; most between-group results lean consistently without being significant alone.
  • Different constructs (Cre-ON vs Cre-OFF) and a twofold brightness difference; amplitude, inferred rate and the light composite are not controlled for construct.
  • Ex vivo cell types come from largely separate mice (effectively 5 vs 2).
  • CASCADE was trained on other cell types; inferred rates partly track brightness.
  • Composites are exploratory, and some measures share data (light-on/off from the same transitions; speed and movement during the same events).
  • Running and visual flow are confounded in a freely moving animal.
  • The overhead image is assumed unmirrored; only left/right labels depend on this, no group comparison does.
  • The mixed-model level of reporting was not computed (statsmodels not installed). Imaging depth and anterior–posterior position are not recorded per session.

Next steps

  1. Image more Penk⁻CamKII+ animals and apply the two fixed composites as the confirmatory test.
  2. Record Penk+ and Penk⁻ cells in the same slices to separate cell type from mouse.
  3. Head-fixed treadmill imaging with controlled visual flow, to separate running from optic flow and light.
  4. Construct-matched labelling (same promoter and indicator, or dual colour in one animal) to remove the brightness confound.
